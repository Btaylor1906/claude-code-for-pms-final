# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

You've now read both folders. Where do they disagree? What's loud in the interviews but rare in the tickets, and what's all over the tickets that nobody brought up in the interviews?

### 2.

For the responders associated with 'phone never goes off' tickets, do they also show higher rates of 'gone before I could answer' events when they do receive a callout? In other words, are these two reported experiences different manifestations of the same underlying dispatch behavior?

### 3.

Comparing the four frequently-mentioned responders (Farlight, The Undertow, Halfmoon, and Corporal Ashgrove) before and after August 12, how did their offered, accepted, declined, missed, and expired callouts change relative to the overall responder population?

### 4.

Looking across interviews, tickets, and post-4.2 changes, which explanation best fits the evidence: notification delivery issues, shorter acceptance windows, ranking/distribution changes, or filter-related visibility problems? What evidence supports and contradicts each hypothesis?
