Rook Dispatch · Release 4.2 review · Fictional teaching scenario

Release 4.2 didn't make responders slower.
It made four of them go silent.

On 12 August the ping wait was cut from 90 seconds to 60 and the ranking was reweighted. Missed pings jumped almost eightfold. The headline numbers recovered. Four responders did not.

What happened

The story in five steps

Source: CLAUDE.md working context, built up over Modules 1 to 5. Data runs 29 Jun to 6 Sep 2026.

12 Aug · Release 4.2
Two changes ship together. Proximity weighted up against recent acceptance, and ping wait cut 90s to 60s. They are confounded, so neither can be judged alone.
12 Aug onward · The misses
Missed pings jump from 2.3% to 18%. About 23% in the first week. Declines stay roughly flat (21.1% to 18.0%). People are not refusing more. They are running out of time.
13 to 14 Aug · The tickets
Support tickets step up to about 4x normal, one to two days after the misses. The wiki says "since 18 Aug", which is late. Two thirds say "phone never goes off", one third "gone before I could answer".
Mid-Aug onward · The slide
Four responders stop being offered pings. A miss costs the same score as a decline, a taken ping earns back less, and nothing ever decays. Their volume falls week after week.
6 Sep · Where the data ends
None of the four has come back. The overall numbers look recovered because the other 12 responders improved and the four dropped out of the totals.

Why the average hid it

Acceptance rate went from 76.6% to 64.0%, then "recovered" to about 75%. That recovery is the other 12 responders (about 10 to 11% missed, still roughly 7x the old 1.5%) plus four responders who no longer appear in the totals. Missed-ping rate is the metric that shows it.

Why it happened

A shorter wait fed a score that never forgives

Best-fit explanation, not proven. Real ranking scores, response times and push delivery have not been seen.

1 · More misses
The 60s wait means more pings time out. The Undertow missed about 1 in 75 before 12 Aug and 5 of his first 7 after.
2 · A miss costs like a decline
−0.12 each time, and only a taken ping gives +0.08 back. With 4.2's weights, proximity counts for more (0.60) and acceptance for less (0.25).
3 · No way back
Low rank means few offers, and no offers means no chance to earn points. All 13 pings the four got after a long gap were missed or turned down.

How each check maps to 4.2

Owner. Every Q3 roadmap item belonged to Priya, who left. Nobody owned the open question on who the weights should apply to.

Success measure. No weekly numbers were presented, and "seasonal" went unchecked. Missed-ping rate with a baseline would have caught it.

Scope. Availability Confidence was committed for 4.2 and quietly missing from the release notes.

Problem first. A long-requested ask for wide-area responders drove the change, but the evidence is one quote and unsourced claims.
